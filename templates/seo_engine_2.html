Maintenance repair

Repair existing published SEO pages

Runs a limited maintenance batch for already published SEO brand pages only. It prioritizes pages missing images, stale or failed cache, external image preservation, and old previews.

Start with 3 brands. Larger batches may take longer because image/cache repair can call existing refresh logic.

This does not publish new pages, promote candidates, generate candidates, delete records, unpublish pages, or change public product flows. It may call the existing SEO cache/image refresh logic.

Existing Apify refresh safety limits still apply, so fewer brands may be refreshed than selected.
